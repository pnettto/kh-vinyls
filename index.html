The archive comes from the PDF lists on kulturhusetstadsteatern.se/lana-hem-vinyl; each record links to the PDF page it came from. Spoken-word records (C numbers) were transcribed from a scanned PDF. Archive records are borrowed by emailing their shelf numbers (use the borrow list). Newer vinyl comes from the catalogue at biblioteket.stockholm.se; borrow those the usual way with your library card, and check availability on each record's catalogue page.
